# Date Whisperer — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2025**

Date Whisperer is a free, open-source Power BI custom visual.

## Data Collection

**Date Whisperer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any user data.**

- No personal information is collected
- No usage analytics or telemetry is sent  
- No network requests are made by the visual
- No cookies are used
- No data leaves your Power BI environment

## Data Processing

The visual only reads date values from the Power BI data model that you explicitly connect to the **Date Field** bucket. This data stays entirely within your Power BI session and is never stored or transmitted.
